About Me

From the Production Floor
To Quality Assurance

Diligent Quality Assurance professional ensuring product excellence, with proven expertise in test planning, execution and defect identification. Collaborates across teams to elevate compliance and operational standards.

I am a dedicated Quality Assurance Assistant and Document Controller with a deep foundation in pharmaceutical manufacturing at Sunshine Holdings Pvt. Ltd. My professional background is unique: before taking on document control and quality systems, I spent seven years directly operating on the production floor.

The Shop-Floor Advantage

Because I have personally operated mixing equipment, executed step-by-step Standard Operating Procedures, and recorded real-time entries in Batch Manufacturing Records under strict cleanroom conditions, I know exactly how documents are used—and where errors can happen. In QA, I turn that hands-on insight into crystal-clear, audit-proof documentation.

Today, I bridge the gap between regulatory mandates and daily operations—resolving BMR/BPR discrepancies to full closure, managing SOP annexure formats, maintaining department logbooks, and governing QMS records, coordinating compliance training, and maintaining pristine QMS records for seamless internal and regulatory audits.

Operational QA Philosophy

  • Uncompromising Traceability: Every raw material lot, equipment check, and environmental factor must be linked to batch records without ambiguity.
  • Real-World Usability: An SOP is only effective if cleanroom technicians can follow it seamlessly under busy operating conditions.
  • Proactive Defect Prevention: Identifying deviations in test planning and in-process line clearances and defect identification before products reach the packing line.
  • Continuous Human Alignment: Utilizing Human Resource Management principles to train teams with patience, clarity, and empathy.
